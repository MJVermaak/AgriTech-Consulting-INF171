Data & Information Management

AgriTech and Food Supply Chain

Understanding Data Management

Data management is the act of gathering data and how it can be stored, maintained and organized. Take a tomatoes farm as an example with three days worth of data. Monday 150kg harvested, Tuesday 140 harvested, Wednesday 160kg harvested. The farmer can use this information/data to try and better help his farm. Asking questions like why Wednesday has the most harvested and why Tuesday has less. The farmer harvested 450kg over the three days this is turning the data into useful information. After gathering the information the farmer uses it to create plans to fix this issue. investigating the workers, checking machinery or farming fields.

Data Across the Supply Chain

Farmers

Farmers can collect information about the quantity harvested, employee/machinery effectiveness and product quality. This information helps because its the root of the whole production figuring this out could help manage what comes next.

Processing Facilities

Processing facilities can use this data to better their way of doing their work. Like if the storage is over flowing or there's not enough delivery vehicle.

Retailers

Retailers can look at the sales and provide feedback, if one farms sales is lower it can be investigated and the problem can be fixed if there is one.

Information Quality

Accuracy

Accuracy helps the farmer make sure the data is in line with reality, the farmer records 450kg harvested while the actual amount was only 50kg.

Completeness

Completeness helps the farmer make sure he has everything recorded, a delivery cant just have the delivery cost but no location to go to

Consistency

Consistency ensures information doesn't collide with each other, you cant record one stock in kg but the other stock in tons and then write everything in kg on your system.

Timeliness

Timeliness ensuring everything is up to date, if an employee changed the total amount that needs to be delivered but set it in wrong or on the wrong date your delivery wont be correct.
